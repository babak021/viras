United States Elite Immigration Gateway

Direct legal pathways to permanent residency (Green Card) and business visas managed by accredited AILA attorneys.

EB-1A Extraordinary Ability & EB-1B Outstanding Researchers

The premier green card category without the requirement of a job offer or Labor Certification (PERM). Designed for individuals who have risen to the very top of their field across sciences, arts, education, business, or athletics.

Self-Petition Advantage

Direct permanent residency for principal applicant, spouse, and children under 21

Premium Processing

Formal USCIS adjudication decision within 15 calendar days

Core Evidentiary Criteria (Meet 3 of 10):

  • Receipt of nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards
  • Membership in associations requiring outstanding achievements
  • Published scholarly material in professional journals and high citation count
  • Participation as a judge of the work of others in the same field
  • Original scientific, scholarly, or business contributions of major significance
  • Command of a high salary or significantly high remuneration

EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW)

An exceptional path to a U.S. Green Card for individuals with an advanced degree (Master's/Ph.D.) or exceptional ability whose proposed endeavor holds substantial merit and national importance to the United States.

Matter of Dhanasar 3-Prong Test

Proving substantial merit, national importance, and applicant readiness to advance the endeavor

No Employer Sponsor Required

Complete waiver of the lengthy PERM labor certification process

USCIS High Priority Fields:

Artificial intelligence, semiconductors, biotechnology, clean energy, quantum computing, aerospace, supply chain resilience, and advanced healthcare innovations.

Premium Processing Available

45-Day Official Adjudication

Form I-140 Premium Processing delivers an official USCIS response within 45 calendar days.

EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program

Under the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act (RIA), invest $800,000 in a qualified Targeted Employment Area (TEA) project or $1,050,000 in non-TEA projects to secure U.S. Green Cards for the investor and their immediate family.

Concurrent Filing Option

Simultaneous filing of I-526E and I-485 for rapid work authorization (EAD) and travel permits

Reserved Visa Set-Asides

20% quota for Rural projects exempt from backlog waiting lines

Vetted Regional Centers

Institutional Capital Security

Direct access to tier-1 Regional Center developers with 100% principal repayment history and Green Card approvals.
